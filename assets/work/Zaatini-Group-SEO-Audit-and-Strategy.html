Finding 1 · Google visibility

Built, finished, and hidden from search.

Every page carries a hidden instruction for search engines. On this site it says "don't list this page and don't follow its links". The setting is usually switched on while a site is being built and forgotten at launch.

Pages Google may list in search All 49 pages checked
Pages on the website49
Pages Google may list0
What I found

One line on every page

From the homepage to the last project, every page includes:

<meta name="robots" content="nofollow, noindex">

It comes from Settings → Reading → "Discourage search engines from indexing this site", which is ticked. Rank Math's own default is already set to Index.

Why it matters

No free traffic, no backup for ads

Until this changes, the site gets no visitors from Google search, and anyone who searches "Zaatini Group" after seeing an ad or a sign won't find the website. Google PageSpeed Insights flags the same issue: "Page is blocked from indexing".

Every other fix in this report depends on this one first.
How to fix

About five minutes

  1. Confirm with the client that the site is officially launched.
  2. Untick "Discourage search engines" in Settings → Reading and save.
  3. Re-check the homepage source for "index, follow" and a canonical tag.
  4. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.

No page currently has a canonical tag (the tag that names a page's official address). Rank Math normally adds it automatically, and it usually leaves it out while pages are set to noindex. Check again after the fix.

Homepage

Four videos start playing at once

The hero showreel is 8.0 MB and three background panels add 2.8 MB, all set to autoplay. The company film is set not to load until pressed, which is right. The 821-pixel logo is also loaded through the old zaatinihomes.com.au domain, adding a redirect.

Compress the showreel to under 2 MB, load the panel videos only when scrolled into view, and serve a small logo from zaatinigroup.com.au.
Service pages

Large photos and no caching

Google estimates 1.9 MB of savings from better image formats on the Knockdown Rebuilds page alone, and asks for longer browser caching. No caching or image-optimisation plugin is installed, and images have no set width and height.

Add a caching plugin and convert images to WebP at display size. Re-test every service page afterwards.

Finding 5 · On-page & technical details

Smaller fixes that add up.

None of these stop the site ranking on their own. Together they decide how well each page competes once Google can see it.

Headings

The homepage heading doesn't say what you do

The main heading is "Crafting Homes, Anchored in Faith". It's a good brand line, but it says nothing about building or Sydney. The Services page has two main headings, and two design pages use all capitals ("PREMIUM 3D PLANS").

Keep the brand line and add a heading such as "Custom Home Builders in Western Sydney". One main heading per page.
Titles & descriptions

Vague titles on listing pages

"News - Zaatini Group", "Reviews - Zaatini Group", "Projects - Zaatini Group" and "Blog - Zaatini Group" don't say what or where. Project pages use the project name only, and four projects share one description.

For example, "Custom Home Projects Western Sydney | Zaatini Group", and a description for each project with suburb and scope.
Structured data

Good base, two gaps

The homepage tells Google the business type, address, phone and opening hours. Service pages only have breadcrumbs. The logo in the markup is the old "Zaatini Homes" mark, and the markup email (micheal@zaatinihomes.com.au) differs from the footer (info@zaatinigroup.com.au).

Add Service markup to each service page, and update the logo and email in Rank Math's Local SEO settings.
Images

Missing alt text

The header logo has empty alt text on every page, and 7 gallery photos on Marlowe House have none. Alt text helps Google Images and screen readers.

Logo alt "Zaatini Group", and short descriptions for project photos, such as "Kitchen, Bella Vista custom home".
Sitemap & links

Tidy what Google is sent

The sitemap lists the two "– old" pages and lists /projects/ twice. The footer of every page links to the WordPress login ("Staff login"). The page language is set to US English.

Remove the old pages from the sitemap, drop the public login link, and set the site language to English (Australia).
Housekeeping

Search Console, updates and access

The homepage carries two Google verification codes, so confirm who owns Search Console. Rank Math, Advanced Custom Fields, Duplicator Pro, GTM4WP and WP Mail SMTP have updates waiting. The WPVibe plugin is set to bypass approvals for AI changes.

Get Search Console access, apply plugin updates after a backup, and confirm the WPVibe setting is intended.

Finding 6 · Local SEO

Based in Jamisontown, competing for all of Sydney.

The business is at Shop 2, 55-61 York Road, Jamisontown NSW 2750, near Penrith. The projects on the site are mostly in the Hills District (Bella Vista, Kellyville, Box Hill, Castle Hill, Winston Hills, Glenhaven) plus Kemps Creek, Cronulla and Greenacre. The site, however, targets "Sydney" as one place.

Google Business Profile

Not confirmed

I couldn't find a Google Business Profile or any public reviews for Zaatini Group. For a builder, the map listing and its reviews often bring more enquiries than the website itself.

Confirm the profile exists, is verified and uses the same name, address and phone as the website.
Area relevance

"Sydney" is too broad

Every title says "Sydney", where the biggest builders compete. A Jamisontown builder has a better chance in Western Sydney, Penrith and the Hills District, where its projects already are.

Name the areas you actually build in, on service pages and in real project write-ups.
Consistency

Two brands, two domains

The old zaatinihomes.com.au domain redirects correctly, but the "Zaatini Homes" name still shows in the structured-data logo and the business email. Directories and Google should all see one name: Zaatini Group.

One name, address and phone everywhere.

SEO strategy

Own Western Sydney first, then grow outward.

The aim is enquiries for high-value builds: custom homes, knockdown rebuilds, duplexes, granny flats and second storeys. The fastest route is to win the areas closest to the business and its past work, then expand. Six pillars, in order of dependency.

01

Make the site visible and clean

Nothing else works until Google can index the site and sees one clear page per topic.

  • Remove noindex, submit the sitemap
  • Retire duplicates, clean addresses
  • Fill or hide empty pages
  • Fix mobile speed
02

Service pages as the main earners

Each service page targets one service plus area, with its own title, description, heading and service markup.

  • Custom homes, knockdown rebuilds
  • Duplex, granny flats, second storeys
  • Renovations, extensions, bathrooms
  • Design services as a supporting group
03

Proof that converts

Real projects and real reviews are what turn a visit into an enquiry, and what Google looks for as evidence of experience.

  • Real project pages: suburb, scope, photos
  • Google reviews shown on the site
  • Named team members and the licence
04

Local reach

Rank in the map results and in the areas that matter, starting with Penrith and the Hills District.

  • Google Business Profile, verified
  • Area pages built from real local projects
  • Consistent listings in trade directories
05

Blog for early-stage searches

Answer the questions people ask months before they choose a builder, and link each article to the matching service page.

  • Cost and timeline guides
  • Approvals and planning explainers
  • Choosing a builder, checking a licence
06

Measure what matters

Track enquiries, not just visits, so effort goes where it pays.

  • Search Console access and monitoring
  • Form and phone enquiries as conversions
  • Monthly ranking and enquiry report

First blog topics

Questions buyers ask early

What a knockdown rebuild costs in Western Sydney, and what's included. Granny flat or second storey: which adds more space for the money. How long a custom home build takes, stage by stage. What to check before signing with a builder, including the licence. Duplex and dual occupancy: where to start.

Area pages

Only where there's real work to show

Start with Penrith (home base) and the Hills District (most listed projects). Each area page features real projects in that area, so it's genuinely useful rather than a copy with the suburb name swapped.

Add more areas as real projects are completed there.
